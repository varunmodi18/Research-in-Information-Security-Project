## v1.0.0 — 2026-10-04

### M8 — Demo, documentation, release

- `python -m maka_server seed-demo` and `reset-demo`. The `reset_demo` job behind Admin → Reset demo,
  and `make demo-seed` / `make demo-reset`, restore exactly "Vineyard" and "Lab-Paper".
- Fixed: two demo resets within one second were merged into one job by a time-based idempotency key.
- Fixed: a deleted network's buffered SSE events could be replayed to a new network that reused its id.
- The Security events page refreshes every 2 s, so events from a running job appear without reloading.
- `docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md` with fallback screenshots in `docs/demo_assets/`, and `web/e2e/demo.spec.ts`,
  which performs the whole script.
- New docs: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/API.md` (generated by `tools/gen_api_doc.py`),
  `docs/OPERATIONS.md` and `docs/USER_GUIDE.md`. Updated: `README.md` and `docs/REGISTER.md`.

### M7 — Formal models and evaluation (I-13, I-14, I-17, P-12)

- The RP9 HLPSL, transcribed exactly, shows defects D1–D7 (OB-09). OFMC checks the AnB models: MAKA-E
  has no attack at 1–3 sessions, while RP9 is attacked by an insider (P-01) and by a cross-session
  replay (OB-10). CL-AtSe and the HLPSL runs were not obtained (OI-02).
- `docs/SECURITY_ARGUMENT.md` and `docs/RESIDUAL_RISKS.md` (P-11 and accepted risks).
- Original-vs-enhanced comparison, primitive regression and API-latency benchmarks
  (V-PERF-01..04). Every §6.6 threshold is met.
- Evaluation page and the `evaluation` job.

### M6 — Attack laboratory (P-01, P-03..P-08, P-10, I-11)

- An adversary on real protocol bytes, and Lab scenarios L1–L8 run in both modes, with persisted
  evidence. All 16 outcomes are as predicted.

### M5 — Product workflows (P-06, P-09, D-01..D-03)

- Pages: Device, Timeline, Frames, Readings and Admin. Jobs: rekey, revoke, reprovision, designate,
  reset network, and periodic readings. Journeys J1–J3 run in E2E.

### M4 — MAKA-E v1 (P-01..P-10, P-12, I-10)

- C1 only the BS holds `k`; C2 SOK pre-shared keys; C3 PSK + ephemeral-DH key exchange; C4 membership,
  designation, revocation and authenticated batching.
- Test vectors in `tests/vectors/maka_e_v1.json` (IA-17).

### M3 — Console skeleton (D-04, D-05, I-09)

- FastAPI, SQLite/Alembic, Argon2id sessions, CSRF, lockout, problem+json errors, audit log, jobs and
  SSE.
- Keystores encrypted under the KEK.

  withdrawn by owner decision: follow-up Part B, E-07.)

### M2 — Device runtime (I-01, I-03, I-04, I-07, I-11)

- A bytes-only runtime: frames, bus with interceptors, scheduler, devices and keystore. RP9's original
  mode re-expressed as state machines on it.

- Falsifiable security checks (I-12), corrected Tables 5–6 (I-13), and documentation fixes
  (I-15, I-16).
